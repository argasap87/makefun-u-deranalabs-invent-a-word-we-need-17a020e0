**Fingerrattle** — the unconscious tapping of a finger against a desk while waiting for something to load.

Everyone does it: the page spins, the lift is late, the kettle hasn't boiled, and your finger starts drumming on its own. English has *fidgeting* and *tapping*, but no single word for this exact waiting-tap — the little percussion of impatience. *Fingerrattle* fills that gap: finger + rattle, sayable, spellable, and instantly recognisable the first time you hear it.

## Run it

MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output node main.ts
